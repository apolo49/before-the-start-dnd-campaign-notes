On the [[Setting-old/Semantics/Dating/index|11th of Ebolumi 821 IOG]], [[Anparashi Puli Kondobonioki Ailushi|Tidi Bralosai Kondobonioki]] is proclaimed emperor by various [[Rikati]] in the East as well as the prefect of [[Campaign 1 - Before the Start/DM Notes/Nations/Admorru]]. Upon ascension he adopts the name *Anparashi Puli Kondobonioki Ailushi*.
