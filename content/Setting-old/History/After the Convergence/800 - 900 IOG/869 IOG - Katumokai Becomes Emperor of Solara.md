Failure to nominate an heir could invite chaotic, destructive wresting of power by a succession of competing claimants – a civil war. Too early a nomination could be seen as an abdication and reduce the chance for an orderly transmission of power. As [[Anparashi Puli Mabosi Floligaiki Ailushi|Floligaiki]] lay dying, nursed by his wife, and closely watched by Prefect Atti, he could have lawfully adopted [[Anparashi Puli Floligaiki Katumokai Ailushi|Katumokai]] as heir by means of a simple deathbed wish, expressed before witnesses; but when an adoption document was eventually presented, it was signed not by Floligaiki but by his wife. That Katumokai was still in [[Province of Pariai|Pariai]] was a further irregularity, as [[Campaign 1 - Before the Start/DM Notes/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] adoption law required the presence of both parties at the adoption ceremony. Rumours, doubts, and speculation attended Katumokai's adoption and succession. It has been suggested that Floligaiki's young manservant, who died very soon after Floligaiki, was killed (or killed himself) rather than face awkward questions. Ancient sources are divided on the legitimacy of Katumokai's adoption: Di saw it as bogus and the another writer as genuine. A Rosisti minted early in Katumokai's reign represents the official position; it presents Katumokai as Floligaiki's "Puli" (Floligaiki's heir designate).

According to a late [[Campaign 1 - Before the Start/DM Notes/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] collection of biographies, written in [[Solari (Language)|Solari]], of the [[Solara|Solari]] emperors, their junior colleagues, designated heirs and usurpers, Katumokai informed the [[Senate of Solara|Senate]] of his accession in a letter as a fait accompli, explaining that "the unseemly haste of the troops in acclaiming him emperor was due to the belief that the state could not be without an emperor". The new emperor rewarded the [[Military of Solara|Rikati]]'s loyalty with the customary bonus, and the Senate endorsed the acclamation. Various public ceremonies were organised on Katumokai's behalf, celebrating his "divine election" by all the gods, whose community now included Floligaiki, deified at Katumokai's request.

On [[Setting-old/Semantics/Dating/index|24th of Bomi, 869 IOG]] Katumokai was proclaimed emperor of all Solara and adopted his name "Anparashi Puli Floligaiki Katumokai Ailushi."
