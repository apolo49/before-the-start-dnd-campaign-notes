On [[Setting-old/Semantics/Dating/index|6th of Atmi 848 IOG]], [[Anparashi Puli Damashaoi Ailushi|Damashaoi]] was assassinated in a palace conspiracy organised by court officials. On the very same day, records state that the [[Senate of Solara]] proclaimed [[Anparashi Mabosi Puli Ailushi|Nariai Kagayai Mabosi]] emperor, taking the name *Anparashi Mabosi Puli Ailushi*. This was the first time the Senate actually chose a new emperor rather than simply ratifying formally a choice made by either a previous emperor in his testament or an army or the [[Solaris Vanguard]]. Despite his political experience, this was a remarkable choice. Mabosi was old and childless, and had spent much of his career out of the public light, prompting both ancient and modern authors to speculate on his involvement in Damashaoi's assassination, although his probable lack of involvement would have made him acceptable to the Damashaoi faction.

Mabosi founded the [[Mabosi-Anatunoki Dynasty]], controlling Solara from 848 IOG to 944 IOG.
