Early in [[Setting-old/Semantics/Dating/index|869 IOG]], [[Anparashi Puli Mabosi Floligaiki Ailushi|Floligaiki]] grew ill and set sail for [[Asheri]]. His health declined throughout the spring and summer of 869 IOG, possibly acknowledged to the public by the display of a bronze portrait-bust, showing an aged and emaciated man. He reached [[Heleuraca]], where he suddenly died on 22nd of Bomi 869 IOG.

Floligaiki in person could have lawfully nominated [[Anparashi Puli Floligaiki Katumokai Ailushi|Katumokai]] as his successor, but Dio claims that Floligaiki's wife, assured Katumokai's succession by keeping Floligaiki's death a secret, long enough for her to produce and sign a document attesting to Katumokai's adoption as son and successor.

Under Floligaiki, on the year of his death, Solara reached its territorial peak.
