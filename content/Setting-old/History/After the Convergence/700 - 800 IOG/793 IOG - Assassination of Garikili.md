On the [[Setting-old/Semantics/Dating/index|21st of Hanomi 793 IOG]], the day before his due departure for [[Campaign 1 - Before the Start/DM Notes/Nations/Admorru]], [[Rai Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Garikili]] was assassinated by several [[Rikati]]. Historians of the time name many of his inner circle as conspirators in the assassination. The [[Senate of Solara|Senate]] was a disunited body of self-interested, wealthy and mistrustful aristocrats, unwilling to risk their own prospects, and determined to present a virtuous, united front.

The Senate tried to use Garikili's death as an opportunity to restore the Republic of [[Solara]]. This would have meant the abolition of the office of emperor, the end of dynastic rule, and restoration of the former social stature and privilege of nobles and senators. Most ordinary citizens were taken aback by Garikili's murder, and found no cause to celebrate in losing the benefits of his rule. Almost all the named conspirators were from the elite.

The assassins, fearing continued support for Garikili's family and allies, sought out and murdered Garikili's wife, and their young daughter, but were unable to reach his uncle [[Atrinoki Grudikali Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Grudikali]]. A sympathetic faction of the [[Solaris Vanguard]] smuggled him out to their nearby camp, and nominated him as emperor. The Senate, faced with what now seemed inevitable, confirmed their choice.

The killing of Garikili had been extralegal, tantamount to regicide, and those who carried it out had broken their oaths of loyalty to him.
