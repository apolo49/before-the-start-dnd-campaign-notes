The region of __Asheri__ is the core region of the [[Solara|Solari Republic]]. It extends from the [[Albi|Albi]] mountain range in the north to the Western edge of the [[Moli Aurirorinao|Moli Aeianao]], following the [[Setting/Landmarks/Sorao Auzi|Sorao Auzi]]. The southern border of the region is defined by being between the two most southernly points of the Moli Aeianao and the [[Moli Ikozinao|Moli Ikozinao]]. The region is also known as the __Solari Core__, or the __Home of Solaum__.

# Overview

Over the middle of the region is the [[Atri|Atri River]]. This is where the bulk of the population sits, within [[Solaum]] and its surrounding satellite villages, towns and cities including [[Condobonia|Condobonia]] and [[Shiki|Shiki]] as well as the port village of [[Polbaridaco|Polbaridaco]]. Being situated on and around the Atri River makes this a strategic trade and battle point as the river is situated on a hill and flows from west to east. It means that Solaum and the other trading settlements along the river get to control all trade and transport between the two seas it connects.

# Cultural Geography

Despite being called the "Home of Solaum" and being entirely owned by [[Solara|Solara]] currently, the Asheri region is a culturally diverse area. Being home to many cultures, most of which are related to the Solari people, it was seen as a place of prosper and riches.
