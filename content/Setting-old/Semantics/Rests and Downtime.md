The campaign will have more resting than normal and so the idea of rests (short and long) will change to a modified version of the Gritty Realism rules:

# Short Rest
Duration: 60 minutes

# Night's Rest (NEW)
Duration: 8 Hours
Same as short rest, but get half expended hit die back

# Long Rest
Duration: 48 hours in good conditions, 72 hours in wilderness
Impossible to long rest in a dungeon.
