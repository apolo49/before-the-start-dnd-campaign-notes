### Limitations on the Above Sourcebooks
Due to the setting of Campaign 2, some aspects of the above sourcebook are limited, especially those related to the artificer and the setting the artificer inhabits. Firearms are not yet invented and without a reasonable explanation they cannot be crafted either. Firearms as of the year the campaign starts ([[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|1782 IOG]]) are still about 300 years from being invented. The earliest a firearm or any black-powder weaponry has been alluded to was in [[Session 16 - The Rumour of Home]], wherein [[Riley - Player Notes|Riley]] was targeted with a fire-lance or otherwise similar weapon that backfired, causing some damage to him while also killing the attacker.
