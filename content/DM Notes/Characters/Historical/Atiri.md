__Atiri__ (350 - 434 [[Dating|IOG]]) was a scholar, mage, and philosopher of [[Solara|the Solari Replublic]] and the founder of the [[Atiri Centre of Magical Excellence]] as well as the author of many texts and grimoires including the [[Atiric Texts]]. He taught by [[Varma]] an [[Setting/Cultures/Aeia|Aeian]] Elf.

Atiri is known to have lived a troubled life. He was born in [[Solaum]], capital of the Solari Republic. His father and mother both died when his house was raided at the age of 7. He was then captured and sold into slavery at [[Itrea]], one of the holiest cities in the world and gateway to [[Mount Aemi]]. There he was taught by Varma the ways of magic and magical studies. This experience moulded his world-view and his outlook on studying. He remained in the tutelage of Varma until he disappeared at age 62. He then travelled back to Solaum to found his academy.

Among his contemporaries and those who knew him he was seen as a man of moderation, but also of great power. Many would freeze upon gazing upon him due to the sheer amount of mana he possessed. His writing was considered abstruse and meandering by his teacher and his students. He was also considered to have a greater understanding of magic than everyone other than Varma, though Varma preferred to be moderate with his utilisation of the craft.

Due to his contributions and legendary life many works are attributed to his name though most are fakes.

# Life
## Early Life
Atiri was born on [[Dating|9th of Minumi 350]] to poor parents in the slums of Solaum outside the [[Buno Walls]]. His parents were not remarkable in any way and he described them in his writings as "dull and uninteresting." His parents were however descendants of the founders of Solaum and thus he was ethnically [[Setting/Cultures/Solari|Solari]]. His birthname is unknown as he had forgotten it shortly after his kidnapping and was referred to as only "horabos" which means slave in [[Aeikas]]. As a result of his upbringing he was fluent in both [[Setting/Languages/Solari|Solari]] and Aeikas. He was given his name by Varma. As far as we know he had no siblings and was an only child, however, we do know all his family died during the kidnapping.

During his childhood Solara was undergoing rapid expansion, conquering neighbouring tribes in [[Setting/Regions/Asheri|Asheri]]. This rapid expansion brought new challenges for Solara including placating the recently conquered peoples and assimilating them into a hostile society. Atiri's father was involved in the war to some unknown degree. Atiri had no formal education growing up in his familial home, however Atiri enjoyed writing, especially journaling and poetry, which he had learned from his mother. He dropped writing poems when he met his teacher, Varma.

Sometime in [[357 - Atiri Gets Captured|357 IOG Atiri's familial home was razed, his family was killed and he was captured]] by some [[Setting/Cultures/Margos|Margos]] peoples. During his capture he was a slave to the travellers who went around pillaging Solari settlements and are known to have travelled east with their captives, looking to make a profit selling their spoils. It is during this time he was sold at the city of [[Setting/Settlements/Cities/Itrea|Itrea]].

In Itrea, Atiri was treated as second class but he had time to be mindful of himself and be true to his religion. Itrea is a holy city and, as the [[Setting/Religions/Solari|Solari Religion]] is highly syncretic, he found time to worship his gods in the various temples scattered around the city. His slave masters called him "[[Setting/Languages/Aeikas|horabos]]" as a derogatory term meaning "slave," due to this treatment he forgot his birthname. He was frequently beat as a form of disciplining which was illegal in Itrea, but he could not speak up because he was not a citizen of the land.

Around the age of 15 when getting beat he discovered he was a [[Setting/Elements/Fire|Fire elemental]] and accidentally burnt down his masters' house. He also caused a small fire in the surrounding area, in the process he killed his masters. To avoid death he escaped Itrea and ran in to the woods where he lived until he found Varma.

## Magical Career
Atiri found himself practicing magic in the forest outside of [[Setting/Settlements/Cities/Itrea|Itrea]] just under [[Mount Aemi]] for two years. Lighting and starting simple fires. When he was found by [[Varma]] he was on the verge of accidentally causing a forest fire because the fire he had lit had starting burning out of control. According to Atiri's own account he was seen as Varma than little more than a nuisance when he first found him and was blamed for putting the forest out of balance and potentially angering the forest's spirit. Atiri found this laughable because he had never heard of the landed - or nature's - spirits before.

### Varmic Teachings
Varma would end up adopting Atiri as his primary apprentice in 367 IOG. During this time Atiri would end up being bestowed his name by his master: "Aetor" in his master's tongue, which means "The One Who Knows" and is transliterated into Solari as _Atiri_.

Varma would teach Atiri everything he deemed him worthy of knowing bit by bit, including magic that has long since been forgotten. This includes nature and draconic magic as well as magic from other elements by transferring his power into him. 

According to Atiri's accounts he Varma would wake him at dawn every day and make him practice magic until the sun set at the end of the day, only stopping to alleviate themselves and eat. Varma would often disappear and hunt for food however, sometimes as though they were nomads rather than settled, civilised beings. He also recounted that it seemed as though Varma already had hundreds, if not thousands of years of untapped knowledge. He also though as though Varma knew something Atiri didn't at all times, also that Varma seemed to have experience in training others other than himself.

#### Ascent of Mount Aemi
Atiri often claimed that Varma would express that being in tune with nature around you during a fight gave you the favour of the spirits of nature and that they would help you out. To be this in tune required that you understood and appreciate the earth for the simplest things.

One day Varma woke Atiri up at an untimely hour before sunrise and told him to follow him up Mount Aemi. On the way up, he said that Varma was recounting how every leaf, every tree and every blade of grass had its own soul, as did the breeze, the moon and each star in the sky and so did the mountain. Atiri was taking in his every word as if he was going to say something different or teach him something revolutionary.

Upon reaching the peak, Varma sat himself on the floor and stared straight towards the direction of the easterly wind, claiming both of them will be able to meet [[Aushuzi]], Goddess of the Dawn, on a day like this. They both watched the sun rise together and while Varma claimed he could feel her presence, Atiri said nothing happened and he couldn't feel anything. Varma said he would not be able to feel anything until he was truly in tune with nature.

Atiri continued to ascend the mountain every morning, before dawn, when the wind blew east. He claimed to have never met Aushuzi before he died.

#### The Disappearance of Varma
On 15th Narimi 412 IOG (The day of the moon of war in the Solari calendar), which corresponds to 1-1-4212 in the [[Aeia Foundation Calendar]] also known as the [[Dark Night Festival]], Atiri woke up and noticed that something was different about where he had been, the world felt empty and there was a notable absence where something once filled the air surrounding the nature in the area. He claimed the plants looked in despair but he couldn't describe how he knew. He noted the wind was also blowing South to North that day and he didn't climb the mountain as something was eerie.

He waited patiently for Varma to come to greet him every day for the past 45 years of his life, but he never showed. He says there was not a trace left behind of his leave and not a footprint in the mud, just an emptiness in the air as if he was never there before and never there after.

It was after this he decided to take what he knew and head back to Solaum.

### Travels
Before heading back to Solaum he realised he needed to earn some money. He then started to perform miracles and barter for food, using hunting, gathering and foraging skills he had learned from Varma to get him between settlements. He tended to not want to stay too long in any one place but had numerous followers by the time he had reached the next road on his path.

He started spreading his learnings and wisdom during this period and spread knowledge in what he called the "Varmic Teachings." He would say to people to meet him in Solaum and said they would know when he arrived, without saying how. This is when he finally understood what it meant to be in touch with nature.

Through his lessons he managed to garner a small fortune, but one large enough that he could construct his academy in Solaum.
