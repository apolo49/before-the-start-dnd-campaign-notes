__Atiri__ (350 - 434 [[Dating|IOG]]) was a scholar, mage, and philosopher of [[Solara|the Solari Replublic]] and the founder of the [[Atiri Centre of Magical Excellence]] as well as the author of many texts and grimoires including the [[Atiric Texts]]. He taught by [[Varma]] an [[Setting/Cultures/Aeia|Aeian]] Elf.

Among his contemporaries and those who knew him he was seen as a man of moderation, but also of great power. Many would freeze upon gazing upon him due to the sheer amount of mana he possessed. His writing was considered abstruse and meandering by his teacher and his students. He was also considered to have a greater understanding of magic than everyone other than Varma, though Varma preferred to be moderate with his utilisation of the craft.

Due to his contributions and legendary life many works are attributed to his name though most are fakes.
