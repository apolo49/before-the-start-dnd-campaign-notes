# Premise
The party, now informed of [[689 - Present - Duleganic Wars|The Duleganic Wars]] and [[Lai Puli Ozi]]'s whereabouts by [[Kaddurix]], set off to pursue the dictator. They are navigating their way out of [[Solaum]] by heading east towards [[Condobonia]], their first major stop. Along the way they stop at a nearby [[Shtaduli ip Oshkaro|hostelry]] within the [[Shkari Surbaiko]] for some rest after a long day of travel.

# Story
The players have now departed the [[Better Future for Solara Head Quarters]] and by extension the [[Trabini ip Odolumika]] and stand in front of the [[Dolumi Solaunao]]. They now are greeted by the beginnings of a market in the forum square in the morning where they can shop for their upcoming travel out of the city. Here they are able to buy some departure papers off of a rogue down a back alley, allowing safe passage out of the [[Buno Walls|Inner Walls]]

They are then prompted to take the north-eastern major road out of the city towards [[Condobonia]]. Following this road leads them to the [[Asolaumk Gardens]] where they will be greeted with another market and a giant temple they are free to explore.

Travelling past they are greeted by guards at the [[Buno Walls|Inner Walls of Buni]] where they are asked for their papers to leave, if they do not have valid papers they will have to fight, out maneuverer or persuade the guards of their intentions.

Having passed the Buno Walls, the party then proceeds to pass the outer [[Rivino Walls]] where they encounter another set of guards asking again for papers to depart [[Solaum]], the party do not have valid papers for this and end up in an engagement. Upon passing this engagement the party must run to a safehouse out of Solaum.

After a tiresome journey the party arrive at the [[Shtaduli ip Oshkaro|hostelry]]. Here they are granted some respite and some food. During their stay here they will hear of some side quests and some information to be given about the ongoing [[689 - Present - Duleganic Wars|Duleganic Wars]] and the advances of [[Lai Puli Ozi]].

The players are reminded that they are still in the territory of [[Solara]] and magic is still illegal when they see a [[Military of Solara|Rikatuni]] arrest another elemental.
# Story Beats

- Party leave the [[Better Future for Solara Head Quarters]] and [[Trabini ip Odolumika]]
- The party do some shopping at the market in-front of the [[Dolumi Solaunao]] in the forum square.
	- The party, here, have the option to buy papers to leave the Inner walls, but not the outer walls. (This is not told to them, they are told that the papers will allow them to leave the inner walls, the outer walls are not mentioned).
		- If they do not buy these papers they have an earlier encounter at the inner walls.
	- They can buy other travelling equipment using the money gifted to them by [[Kaddurix]].
- The party pass the [[Asolaumk Gardens]], there is market day on here too. The wares are largely similar, but more expensive.
	- There is also a temple here where they can learn more about the [[Solari Religion]].
- Continuing along the road leads them to the [[Buno Walls]].
	- They are asked for papers to leave here. If they cannot present them they have three options:
		- Bribe: Any bribe in excess of 6 [[Rosist]] (equivalent to 3 Gold) is considered acceptable (this is the same price as the amount of money the entire party is given minus the price of the papers). This can be lowered with a Charisma DC of 22 (the guard will be either Hostile or Indifferent to the party).
			- Intimidation would not be an acceptable choice here. The guards are simply too disciplined, experienced and battle hardened to become intimidated by lowly adventurers.
		- Fight: Enter combat, they are not killed in this combat but are instead captured and imprisoned.
		- Leave: The party can leave and can attempt to climb the walls, in essence circumnavigating the guards.
- They pass the numerous, less fortunate, slums of [[Solaum]] on the way out. They then encounter the [[Rivino Walls]].
	- Here they face an encounter with a set of guards, ensuring no illegals enter or leave Solaum, the prize jewel of [[Solara]].
	- In this encounter they have to fight and flee. They cannot reasonably beat these guards as they wear enchanted armour.
- While fleeing the characters are under pressure to keep running. They then end up at the [[Shtaduli ip Oshkaro|hostelry]] where they can accept various side quests, check the notice board and talk to fellow travellers.
	- Some travellers are [[Dulegan]] refugees and have stories to tell about the war and the current whereabouts and route of [[Lai Puli Ozi]] and his [[Military of Solara|rikati]].
- Once done talking to everyone two [[Military of Solara|rikatuni]] burst in and demand to know the whereabouts of a particular traveller and say they need to arrest him as he is an elemental. The elemental stands and begins to attack them with fire, but he is immediately countered, slammed to the floor and dragged away.
