# Premise
The party, now informed of [[689 - Present - Duleganic Wars|The Duleganic Wars]] and [[Lai Puli Ozi]]'s whereabouts by [[Kaddurix]], they set off to pursue the dictator. They are navigating their way out of [[Solaum]] by heading east towards [[Condobonia]], their first major stop. Along the way they stop at a nearby [[Shtaduli ip Oshkaro|hostelry]] within the [[Shkari Surbaiko]] for some rest after a long day of travel.

# Story
The players have now departed the [[Better Future for Solara Head Quarters]] and by extension the [[Trabini ip Odolumika]] and stand in front of the [[Dolumi Solaunao]]. They now are greeted by the beginnings of a market in the forum square in the morning where they can shop for their upcoming travel out of the city. Here they are able to buy some departure papers off of a rogue down a back alley, allowing safe passage out of the [[Buno Walls|Inner Walls]]

They are then prompted to take the north-eastern major road out of the city towards [[Condobonia]]. Following this road leads them to the [[Asolaumk Gardens]] where they will be greeted with another market and a giant temple they are free to explore.

Travelling past they are greeted by guards at the [[Buno Walls|Inner Walls of Buni]] where they are asked for their papers to leave, if they do not have valid papers they will have to fight, out maneuverer or persuade the guards of their intentions.

Having passed the Buno Walls, the party then proceeds to pass the outer [[Rivino Walls]] where they encounter another set of guards asking again for papers to depart [[Solaum]], the party do not have valid papers for this and end up in an engagement. Upon passing this engagement the party must run to a safehouse out of Solaum.

After a tiresome journey the party arrive at the [[Shtaduli ip Oshkaro|hostelry]]. Here they are granted some respite and some food. During their stay here they will hear of some side quests and some information to be given about the ongoing [[689 - Present - Duleganic Wars|Duleganic Wars]] and the advances of [[Lai Puli Ozi]].

The players are reminded that they are still in the territory of [[Solara]] and magic is still illegal when they see a [[Military of Solara|Rikatuni]] arrest another elemental.
