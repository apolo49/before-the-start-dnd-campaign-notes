# Overview

"Tara" is a Dungeons and Dragons setting and world made by Joe Targett for his group of friends who play DnD together. It now houses 2 separate campaigns. Tara was created to expand on the world that Tegan had created in her homebrew campaign and offer a new enjoyable experience while retaining a similar setting and lore.

Before the Start offers new expansive lore in a world much more expansive than it seems with _thousands_ of years of detailed history, interactive maps and journey planners and much more!
