>> _Main Article: \[[[Session 7 - Pithertara, the WBO, a Strong Man and a Sparkly Bard]]\]_
>> Upon arriving in the bustling port city of [[Campaign 2 - Shadows of the East/Player Notes/Settlements/Pithertara|Pithertara]], Ellowen, Riley, Bob, Yvaquis find themselves swept up in a massive strongman exhibition hosted by the [[World Bodybuilding Order]]. After a botched theft by Riley ruins a local butcher's stall, the group clashes with the reigning strongman champion, the arrogant John Ironchest. However, their initial animosity turns into a reluctant alliance when they discover that [[Elements#Elemental Stones|their mysterious, ancient magical stones]] glow in perfect sympathy with [[Elements#Power Stone|Ironchest's own stone]]. Using Bob's unique brand of negotiation, the crew manages to secure [[Manuscript on Tabaxi|a burned Imperial manuscript from the champion detailing the tragic and hidden history of the rare Tabaxi race]], a revelation that deeply affects Riley.
>> As the day progresses, the party expands their ranks by recruiting Rhoda, an eccentric merfairy bard, and Ironchest himself, who reveals he experienced a prophetic vision perfectly mirroring the recent murder of the [[Mari Shkari|crew's captain]]. While securing vital provisions for their ongoing voyage to [[City State of Itrea|Itrea]] and adopting a tiny gelatinous cube named [[Woubble]], Ellowen covertly gathers crucial intelligence from local Solari guards. She learns startling political secrets regarding highly-ranked Tabaxi within [[Empire of Solara|the empire]] and her own impending arranged marriage to [[The Prince - Player Notes|the Prince of Amphos]], setting the stage for their treacherous journey ahead.
