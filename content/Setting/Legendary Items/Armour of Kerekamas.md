Location: [[Ebbu]], [[Tomb of Kerekamas]]
AC: 18
Weight: 45
Strength Requirement: 13
The armour gains a resistance based and bonus on the elemental power of the wearer:

| Element of Wearer | Resistance  | Bonus                                                |
| ----------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Fire              | Fire        | Can cast _Flaming Sphere_ without using a spell slot |
| Ice               | Cold        | Can cast _Ice Knife_ without using a spell slot      |
| Water             | Poison      | Can breathe indefinitely under water                 |
| Earth             | Thunder     | All melee attacks are bludgeoning                    |
| Soul              | Psychic     | All psychic spells have advantage                    |
| Air               | Bludgeoning | Take no fall damage                                  |
| Power             | Force       | All attack rolls have a +1 bonus                     |
| Electricity       | Lightning   | All melee attacks and spells deal lightning damage   |
