Location: [[Ectia]], [[Inverbegled]], [[Harrow of Lug]]
Rarity: Unique
Type: Wonderous Item
Attunement: Yes: Wizard, Druid, Warlock

**Scales of Lug**, unique item, must be held in a single hand. When used, as an action, the scales will measure the users magical ability against another creature's by weighing their souls on the scale. The creature who has greater magical ability gains complete control over the other.

Magical ability is measured by the amount of available spell slots. Each creature under the control of the scales control occupies an entire spell slot of the holder of the **Scales of Lug** for the duration of the control.

While the target is under the control of another being you have a telepathic link as long as you and the target are on the same plane of existence. You have complete and precise control over the target and you can issue commands to the target which it will do its best to obey however it sees fit. This will constitute the targets' turn(s).

Each turn a creature under the control of the scales can make a Wisdom ability check (DC 28)
