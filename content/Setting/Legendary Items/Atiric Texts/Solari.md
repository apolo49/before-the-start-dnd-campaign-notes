# I. PRINOSAK ONKRAIKO AUINJO

PRINOSUM ONUMO OMOSHKA IBAKA SHA.
NUMI IDOTU, SHIT IINITAZU, SHOT KI IUBANO, UNO INITI.
DATANATUMINIO KALURUIOR GAM NUMI KIOD MADIRI AUINJO AUIN MANOSI IGINOMUMIKO AUINJO SHUSURIZ NAMINA.
NAMIR DANIGINOZO PRINOSUM DASHARI; KRUTIMOZ PIDARUSAK SHI.

DA [[setting/settlements/Cities/Solaum/Buno Walls|OMARO BUNO]], BRITAR PALU KI UBO ISH IGUMGARIZ ONSHAIR SHKIAZ,
ITA DRABIDI [[Setting/Settlements/Cities/Solaum|SURBAIKO]] KIN AN ARODARAAK OBARDZIKO GABITIZ;
KIRU KIM RANKOT JITOANAAK SHOT IDARINIU KRADAZ.
BARININIU AUINJO MASUSAKLINU SHIJZI, AUIN DAKIJZI.
PAUROT DA ISH, BRITAR GOSHINI GIDINOK ISH KITIO NAMINA.
ITA BIROT IARAGOT SHA AUIN TA PAUROT NAMINA.
KO [[Setting/Cultures/Margos|MARGINII]] IATO AUINJO JIRUPIJZI, ZAU LONLIKAZ
BUTINO ANPARAMINOKI IA MIRTO ISH DAKRIAZ.

SIPIT ATNINIU IGINOM SHAZ.
SITOT AOTIKIKO AP TROSARU, SHIT ZAU SITOT AP NILITO ILONLIKALAK.
SHOITA KOTRITA SIMLOT DAKMAZ.
