Following the assassination of [[Lai Puli Ozi]], [[Aelius]], of the original party from the time period 2200 [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|IOG]], transported back by the gods to usher the [[Second Convergence]], as second-in-command to the former dictator-for-life, becomes emperor of [[Solara]] after reuniting a disjointed empire following the death of the party of the elementals after completing the ascension to prevent the [[Second Convergence]] just 10 years prior.

The empire was in revolt after the turmoil caused by the party that instead instigated the convergence and re-introduced magical creatures and magic to the land in swathes. Yet the magic from it it is still below pre-convergence levels.

Aelius becomes emperor by collecting various titles and naming himself after the second monarch of the Kingdom of [[Solara]], "Puli." He was able to do this by formalising his adoption by Lai Puli Ozi posthumously, all heirs of his dynasty (the [[Ailushi Dynasty|Ailushi dynasty]]) were granted this name. With [[Sipiti Pauri Puli Ailushi|Pauri]], who was the first Puli not of the Ailushi dynasty, *[[Puli]]* involves into a formal title and all emperors bear the name. He renames himself **Puli Ailushi Libisai**.

Ailushi created his title by consolidating the constitutional powers of several Republican Solari offices:

- [[Brili Shanmi]]
- [[Arksi Shilo]]
- [[Prinosum Solauni]] (first Solari Person)
- [[Anrpram Gonshuli]] (Imperial Consul)
- [[Bari Kirok]] (Father of the Country)
