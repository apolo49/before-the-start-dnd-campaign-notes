On the [[Dating|5th of Atmi 766 IOG]], [[Atrinoki Rai Puli Ailushi|Atrinoki]] called the [[Senate of Solara|Senate]] in order to validate his position as _Prinosum Solauni_, and, as had [[Aelius|Ailushi]] before him, grant himself its powers. Atrinoki already had the administrative and political powers of the _Princeps_, but he lacked the titles of _[[Ailushi]]_ and _[[Bari Kirok]]_ ("Father of the country"). Like Ailushi before him, Atrinoki may have sought to represent himself as a reluctant yet devoted public servant, no more than an ordinary citizen who wanted to serve the state and people to the best of his ability, but his refusal of these titular, quasi-religious honours, and his reluctance to accept the full powers of a _Prinosum Solauni_ were taken as insults to the elite who offered them; signs of hypocrisy, not humility. Antagonism between Atrinoki and his senate seems to have been a feature of his rule. In his first few years as emperor, Atrinoki seems to have wanted the Senate to act alone, with no reference to him or his responsibilities as "first Senator". His direct orders were rather vague, inspiring debates on what he actually meant, rather than passing his legislation.
