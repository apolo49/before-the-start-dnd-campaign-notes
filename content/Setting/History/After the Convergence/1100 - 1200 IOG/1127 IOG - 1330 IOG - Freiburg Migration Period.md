**The Freiburg Migration Period**, Also known as the **Barbaric Migration Period** to people within the empire of [[Solara]], was a period in history marked by large scale migrations of [[Freiburg]] peoples that saw the [[Fall of the Western Solaran Empire|fall of the Western Solaran Empire]] and the settlement of Freiburg peoples in its former territory leading to the establishment of post-imperial kingdoms there.

The term refers to the important role played by the migration, invasion, and settlement of various tribes, notably the [[Bieiamten]], [[Lieden]], [[Ehrstadt]], [[Gag]], [[Briler]], [[Hus]], [[Bleduro]] within or into the territories of the continent of [[Shul]] as a whole and of the [[Western Solaran Empire]] in particular. Various factors contributed to this phenomenon of migration and invasion.

The beginning of the period is in 1127 [[Dating|IOG]] with the invasion of Shul by the [[Hus]] from [[As]] and ending with the [[Pemiripo]] conquest of [[Asheri]] in 1330 IOG. For example in 1055 IOG, the then Puli of Solara, Puli [[Kontuni]] settled a very large group of [[Lieden]] as foreign mercenaries within the Solari Western regions, and the [[Briler]] were settled just east of them in former [[Dulegan]] territories. In 1168 IOG, a particularly large and unexpected [[1158 IOG - Crossing of the Albi|crossing of the Albi]] was made by a group of [[Herbachia]]. As central power broke down in the Western Solaran Empire, the military of Solara became more important but was dominated by men of barbarian (non-Solari) origin.

The fall of the Western Solaran Empire, although it involved the establishment of competing barbarian kingdoms, was to some extent managed by the [[Eastern Solaran Empire|Eastern emperors]] in [[Amphos]].

Individual migrating groups varied considerably in size, from war bands of a few thousand to entire tribal groups numbering over 100,000. the [[1128 IOG - Lieden Crossing of the Boltachried|Lieden crossing of the Boltachried in 112]] brought 150,000 people into the Empire; The Albi crossing of 1158 IOG involved a coalition of Herbachia, and various Eastern tribes resulting in up to 200,000 people; the Lieden under [[Teodiki the Conqueror|Teodiki]] numbered 150,000 when they invaded Asheri in 1241 IOG. The Eivian migration to [[Butiani]] number to 115,000, while the [[1320 IOG - Pemiripo Invasion of Asheri|Pemiripo invasion of Asheri in 1320]] are at 150,000. Total migration numbers of barbarians in to Solari territory are estimated to range in the hundreds of thousands to low millions.

# Chronology
## Freiburg Tribes Prior to Migration
[[Freiburg]] peoples moved out of Southern [[Unshamia]] and Northern Freiland to the adjacent lands between the [[Boltachried]] and [[Vogtsterken]] after 257 BOG. The first wave moved westward and southward (pushing the resident [[Dulegan]] west to the [[Schopbach]] around 543 IOG) moving into southern Freiland up to the Solari provinces of [[Dulagi]] and [[Brialbi Dulagi]] by 640 IOG, where they were stopped by [[Rai Puli Ozi]] and later by [[Lai Puli Ozi]]. It is this western group which was described by the Roman historian [[Kitii]] and Lai Puli Ozi. A later wave of Frieburg tribes migrated eastward and southward from Unshamia, between 140 IOG and 440 IOG, to the opposite coast of the [[Brae Sea]], moving up the [[Hordh]]. During [[Kitii]]'s era they settled many lesser tribes, however a period of intermarriage and federation resulted in many familiar tribes such as the [[Gag]] and [[Briler]].

## First Wave
The first wave of invasions, between 1127 IOG and 1230 IOG, is partly documented by [[Setting/Cultures/Aeia|Aeia]] and [[Setting/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] historians. It puts Freiburg peoples in control of most areas of what was then the [[Western Solaran Empire]].

Some Freiburg tribes crossed the [[Boltachried]] into Solari territory in 1138, in a migration fleeing the [[Hus]]. Some time later in [[Theorydori]], the escort to their leader [[Bedigern]] was killed while meeting with Solari commander [[Shubiki]]. The [[Lieden]] rebelled, and they eventually invaded Asheri and [[1172 IOG - Sack of Solaum|sacked Solaum in 1172 IOG]] before settling in Dulagi. In 1127 IOG they founded the [[Lieden Kingdom]] in [[Agrisi]]. They were followed into Roman territory first by a confederation of Freiburg warriors from other tribes under [[Adokeri]], that deposed [[Puli Solausi Numini]] in 1228 IOG, and later by the [[Osterlieden]], led by [[Teodiki the Conqueror|Teodiki the Conqueror]], who settled in Asheri.

In [[Dulagi]], the [[Briler]] entered Solari lands gradually during the 1200s IOG and after consolidating power under [[Kildaliki]] and his son [[Grobisi]]'s decisive victory over [[Shiagri]] in 1238, establishing them as the rulers of northern Solari Dulagi. Fending off challenges from other Freiburg tribes such as the [[Lieden]] and [[Abe]], the [[Briler Kingdom]] became the nucleus of what would later become [[Brilia]] and [[Freidland]]. 

The initial [[Eivian settlement of Butiani]] occurred during the 1200s IOG, when [[Solari Butiani|Solari control of Butiani]] had come to an end.

## Second Wave
Between 1250 IOG and 1450 IOG, [[Bleduro]] settled more areas of central Shul and pushed farther into southern and eastern Shul, gradually making the eastern half of Shul predominantly Bleduro-speaking. The [[Pemiripo]] settled in Asheri with their allies in the 1300s IOG. They were later followed by the [[Hertenwe]] and the [[Briler]], who conquered and ruled most of the Italian peninsula.

The [[Krozin]], originally a nomaid group probably from central [[As]], occupied the steppe north of the Eastern [[Aeikas]] kingdoms from the 900s IOG. Later pushed by the [[Kufa]], the majority of them migrated west and dominated territories just north of the [[Amphosian Despotate|Eastern Solaran Empire]], along the lower [[Boltachried]] in the 1340s IOG. From that time the demographic picture of the region changed permanently, becoming predominantly a new language, Bleduro-speaking, while massive populations of native Aeikas-Solari people survived in the mountains of the region.

During the early [[Amphosian-Amkicsh Wars]], [[Lasidan Army|Amkicsh armies]] attempted to invade southeast Shul via southern lands between 1430 IOG and 1470 IOG but were defeated at the [[1469 IOG - 1470 IOG - Siege of Amphos|Seige of Amphos]] by the joint forces of Amphos and the Krozin. The [[Surian|Surians]] were invading Shul from the Southwest [[Surian Conquest of the Egrisi Peninsula|conquering Egrisi]] from the [[Lieden Kingdom]] in 1463 IOG, before being halted at the [[1484 IOG - Battle of Hebhirkunum|Battle of Hebhirkunum]] in Dulagi.
