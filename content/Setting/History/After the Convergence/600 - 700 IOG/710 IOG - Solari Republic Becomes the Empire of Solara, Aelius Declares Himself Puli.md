[[Aelius]], of the original party, becomes emperor of [[Solara]] after the death of the party of the elementals after completing the ascension to prevent the [[Second Convergence]] just 10 years prior, they instead instigated the convergence and re-introduced magical creatures and magic to the land in swathes, yet it is still below pre-convergence levels. He does this by collecting various titles and naming himself after the second monarch of the Kingdom of [[Solara]], "Puli." All emperors of Solara following him call themselves [[Puli]]. He renames himself **Puli Ailushi Libisai**.

The titles he collected are:
- [[Brili Shanmi]]
- [[Arksi Shilo]]
- [[Prinosum Solauni]] (first Solari Person)
- [[Anrpram Gonshuli]] (Imperial Consul)
- [[Bari Kirok]] (Father of the Country)
