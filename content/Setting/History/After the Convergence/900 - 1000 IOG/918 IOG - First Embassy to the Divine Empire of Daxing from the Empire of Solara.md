On the [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|4th of Minumi 918 IOG]] the first ambassadorial mission of [[Setting/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] to the [[Divine Empire of Daxing]] was recorded as having arrived in their capital of [[Lilinlu]]. This was recorded by [[Linhua]] scholars of the time. The embassy came to [[Emperor Liuxian of Daxing|Emperor Liuxian]] from "Anadun" (Linhua: 安啊敦 **ānādūn** for *Anatun*-oki, either [[Anparashi Puli Dati Alaui Katumokai Anatunoki Ailushi Puos|Anatunoki Puos]] or [[Anparashi Puli Narai Azunaki Anatunoki Ailushi|Narai Azunaki Anatunoki]]), "king of Daxibu" ([[Solara]]). Texts state that it was the first time there had been direct contact between the two countries.

As Anatunoki Puos died in 913 IOG, leaving the empire to his adoptive son [[Anparashi Puli Narai Azunaki Anatunoki Ailushi|Narai Azunaki Anatunoki]], and the envoy arrived in 918 IOG, confusion remains about who sent the mission, given that both emperors were named "Anatunoki". The Solari mission came from the south, entering Daxing by their southern frontier. It brought presents of rhinoceros horns, ivory, and tortoise shell, probably acquired in Southern [[As]].
