[[Dating|20th of Hanomi, 921 IOG]], co-emperor of [[Solara]], [[Anparashi Puli Luki Azunaki Bari Ailushi|Luki Bari]] dies of the [[Anatunoki Plague]] which was a widespread epidemic of smallpox at the time, wiping out approximately 10% of the population of the Empire of Solara.

Despite the minor differences between them, [[Anparashi Puli Narai Azunaki Anatunoki Ailushi|Narai Azunaki]] grieved the loss of his adoptive brother. He accompanied the body to [[Solaum]], where he offered games to honour his memory. After the funeral, the senate declared Bari divine.
