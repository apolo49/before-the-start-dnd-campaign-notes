Two [[Rikati]] refused to give their allegiance to Emperor [[Anparashi Sharini Zulbiti Kalpi Puli Ailushi|Kalpi]] on the [[Dating|1st of Hanomi 821 IOG]] and through these a military revolution was speedily accomplished. [[Atni Badipora Fraidaki Anparashi Ailushi|Badipora]] was proclaimed emperor at [[Rheingen]] on the following day, and then again on the day after. More accurately, he was proclaimed emperor of the armies of [[Fraidkiri Pauri]] and [[Fraidkiri Makosa|Makosa]]. The armies of [[Dulagi]], [[Butiani]] and [[Divikai]] sided with them shortly afterwards. By the time that they marched on [[Solaum]], however, it was [[Anparashi Nariai Muii Puli Ailushi|Mulii]], and not Kalpi, whom they had to confront.

In fact, he was never acknowledged as emperor by the entire Solari world, though at Solaum the [[Senate of Solara|Senate]] accepted him and decreed to him the imperial honours on 22nd of Minumi, 821 IOG. He advanced into [[Asheri]] at the head of a licentious and rough soldiery, and Solaum became the scene of riot and massacre, gladiatorial shows and extravagant feasting. To reward his victorious [[Rikati]], Badipora expanded the existing [[Solaris Vanguard]] and installed his own men from his [[Freiburg]] army.
