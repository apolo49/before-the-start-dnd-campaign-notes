To celebrate the construction of the [[Bralosai Amphitheatre]] in [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|832 IOG]], [[Anparashi Tidi Puli Kondobonioki Ailushi|Tidi]] hosted 100 days of games. At the closing of these games, in 833 IOG, Tidi officially dedicated the amphitheatre and the baths in what was his final recorded act as Emperor. He set out for the [[Vincum]] territories but fell ill at the first posting station. 

Tidi had ruled the Roman Empire for just over two years: from the death of his father in 831 IOG to his own on 1st of Atmi 833 IOG. He was succeeded by [[Anparashi Puli Damashaoi Ailushi|Damashaoi]], whose first act as emperor was to deify his brother.
