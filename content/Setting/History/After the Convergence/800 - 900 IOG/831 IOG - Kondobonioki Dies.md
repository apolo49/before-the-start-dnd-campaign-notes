On the [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|3rd of Ebolumi 831 IOG]], [[Anparashi Puli Kondobonioki Ailushi|Kondobonioki]], develops an illness in [[Cetum]], returning at once to [[Solaum]], and then he left for the hot-springs between [[Shiki]] and [[Solaum]], where he spent every summer. However, his illness worsened as he developed severe diarrhoea.

He could feel his life leaving his body, whence he said "An emperor ought to die standing," and struggled to his feet, he died in the arms of those helping him up.
