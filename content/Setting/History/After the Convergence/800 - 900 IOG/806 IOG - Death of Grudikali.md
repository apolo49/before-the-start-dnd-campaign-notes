[[Atrinoki Grudikali Puli Ailushi Freidaki]] was poisoned, possibly contained in mushrooms or on a feather to induce vomiting, and died in the early hours of [[Dating|3rd of Irnumi 806 IOG]]. Nearly all implicate his final and powerful wife as the instigator. Him as his wife had become more combative in the months leading up to his death. This carried on to the point where Grudikali openly lamented his bad wives, and began to comment on his son approaching manhood with an eye towards restoring his status within the imperial family.

Claudius's ashes were interred in the [[Mausoleum of Ailuhsi]] on 14th of Irnumi 806 IOG, after a funeral similar to that of his great-uncle [[Aelius|Ailushi]] 40 years earlier.
