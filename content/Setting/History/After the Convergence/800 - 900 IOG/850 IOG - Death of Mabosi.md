On [[Dating|1st Hanomi 850 IOG]], at the start of his fourth consulship, [[Anparashi Mabosi Puli Ailushi|Mabosi]] suffered a stroke during a private audience. Shortly thereafter he was struck by a fever and died at his villa in the Gardens of Zarusti, on 24th of Hanomi 850 IOG. Mabosi was succeeded without incident by his adopted son [[Anparashi Puli Mabosi Floligaiki Ailushi|Floligaiki]], who was greeted by the Roman populace with much enthusiasm.
