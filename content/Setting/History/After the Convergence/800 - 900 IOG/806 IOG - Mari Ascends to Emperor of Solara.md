[[Mari Grudikali Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Mari]] becomes emperor on 3rd of Irnumi 806 at age 16. His tutor, [[Labisi Amishi Shinaki|Shinaki]], prepared Mari's first speech before the [[Senate of Solara|Senate]]. During this speech, Mari spoke about "eliminating the ills of the previous regime". His respect for Senatorial autonomy, which distinguished him from [[Rai Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Garikili]] and [[Atrinoki Grudikali Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Grudikali]], was generally well received by the Solari Senate.
