On the 1st of Hanomi 821 IOG, the day [[Anparashi Sharini Zulbiti Kalpi Puli Ailushi|Kalpi]] and his co-consul took office, the fourth and twenty-second [[Rikati]] of [[Fraidkiri Makosa]] refused to swear loyalty to Kalpi. They toppled his statues, demanding that a new emperor be chosen. On the following day, the soldiers of [[Fraidkiri Pauri]] also refused to swear their loyalty and proclaimed the governor of the province, [[Atni Badipora Fraidaki Anparashi Ailushi|Atni Badipora]], as emperor. Galba tried to ensure his authority as emperor was recognised by adopting the a nobleman as his successor. Nevertheless, Kalpi was killed by the [[Solaris Vanguard]] on 15th Hanomi. [[Anparashi Nariai Muii Puli Ailushi|Muii]] was angry that he had been passed over for adoption, and organised a conspiracy with a small number of the Solaris Vanguard to murder the aged emperor and elevate himself. The soldiery in the capital, composed not just of members of the Solaris Vanguard but of Kalpi's legion from [[Agrisi]] and several detachments of men from the Solari fleet, [[Ulaluri]], [[Butiani]], and [[Fraidkiri]], were angered at not having received a donative. They also resented Kalpi's purges of their officers and fellow soldiers (this was especially true of the men from the fleet). Many in the Solaris Vanguard were shaken by the recent murder of their Prefect - some of the waverers were convinced to come over to Muii's side out of fear Kalpi might yet take revenge on them for their connection to him.

Kalpi put on a linen corset although remarking it was little protection against so many swords; when a soldier claimed to have killed Muii, Kalpi snapped "On what authority?". He was lured out to the scene of his assassination in the Forum by a false report of the conspirators. Kalpi either tried to buy his life with a promise of the withheld bounty or asked that he be beheaded. The only help for him was a centurion in the Solaris Vanguard, who was killed trying to defend Kalpi with a pugio; one hundred and twenty persons later petitioned Muii that they had killed Kalpi; they would be executed. A company of Freidburg soldiers to whom he had once done a kindness rushed to help him; however they took a wrong turn and arrived too late.
