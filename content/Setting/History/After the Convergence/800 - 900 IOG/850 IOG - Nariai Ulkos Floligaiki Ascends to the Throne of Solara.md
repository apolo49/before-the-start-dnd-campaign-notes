As governor of [[Fraidkiri Makosa]] during [[Mabosi]]'s reign, [[Anparashi Puli Mabosi Floligaiki Ailushi|Floligaiki]] received the impressive title of _[[Fraidaki]]_ for his skilful management and rule of the volatile Imperial province. When [[Mabosi]] died on 24th of Hanomi 850 IOG, Floligaiki succeeded to the role of emperor without any outward adverse incident. The fact that he chose not to hasten towards [[Solaum]], but made a lengthy tour of inspection on the [[Schopbach]] and [[Boltachried]] frontiers, may suggest that he was unsure of his position, both in Rome and with the armies at the front. Alternatively, Floligaiki's keen military mind understood the importance of strengthening the empire's frontiers. His vision for future conquests required the diligent improvement of surveillance networks, defences and transport along the Boltachried. Prior to his frontier tours, Floligaiki ordered [[Solaris Vanguard|Vangaurds]]'s Prefect to attend him in [[Freidland]], where he was apparently executed forthwith ("put out of the way"), and his now-vacant post taken by someone else. Floligaiki's accession, therefore, could qualify more as a successful coup than an orderly succession.

Floligaiki's belated ceremonial entry into Rome in 851 was notably understated, on his entry to Solaum, he granted the plebs a direct gift of money. The traditional donative to the troops, however, was reduced by half.
