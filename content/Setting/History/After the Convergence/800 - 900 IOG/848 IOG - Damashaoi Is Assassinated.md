[[Anparashi Puli Damashaoi Ailushi|Damashaoi]] was assassinated on [[Setting/Semantics/Dating/index|6th of Atmi 848 IOG]] in a conspiracy by court officials. Damashaoi's chamberlain played the main role in the plot, and his likely fear over Damashaoi's recent execution of [[Mari]]'s former secretary as a possible motive. The act itself was carried out by a freedman of the chamberlain's, and a steward of Damashaoi's niece. A number of omens had foretold Damashaoi's death.

According to an auspice he had received, the Emperor believed that his death would be at midday. As a result, he was always restless around that time. On the day of the assassination, Damashaoi was distressed and repeatedly asked a servant to tell him what time it was. The servant, who was himself one of the plotters, lied to the emperor, telling him that it was already late in the afternoon. Apparently put at ease, the Emperor went to his desk to sign some decrees. The chamberlain, who had been feigning an injury to his arm for several days and wearing a bandage to allow him to carry a concealed dagger, suddenly appeared and stabbed him eight times.
