On the [[Dating|17th of Garimi 820 IOG]], Emperor [[Mari Grudikali Puli Ailushi Freidaki|Mari]] commits suicide by means of assisted suicide through his private secretary.

The night before Mari had returned to [[Solaum]] and spent the evening in the palace. After sleeping, he awoke at about midnight to find the palace guard had left. Dispatching messages to his friends' palace chambers for them to come, he received no answers. Upon going to their chambers personally, he found them all abandoned. When he called for a gladiator or anyone else adept with a sword to kill him, no one appeared. He cried, "Have I neither friend nor foe?" and ran out as if to throw himself into the [[Atri]]. Returning, Mari sought a place where he could hide and collect his thoughts. An imperial freedman, Zaoni, offered his villa, 6.5 km outside the city. Travelling in disguise, Mari and four loyal freedmen reached the villa, whereupon Mari ordered them to dig a grave for him. At this time, Mari learned that the [[Senate of Solara|Senate]] had declared him a public enemy. Mari prepared himself for suicide, pacing up and down muttering. Losing his nerve, he begged one of his companions to set an example by killing himself first. At last, the sound of approaching horsemen drove Mari to face the end as he ordered his private secretary to kill him.

When one of the horsemen entered and saw that Mari was dying, he attempted to stop the bleeding, but efforts to save Mari's life were unsuccessful. Mari's final words were "Too late! This is fidelity!" With Mari's death, the [[Ailushi Dynasty]] ended. Chaos would ensue in the Year of the Four Emperors.
