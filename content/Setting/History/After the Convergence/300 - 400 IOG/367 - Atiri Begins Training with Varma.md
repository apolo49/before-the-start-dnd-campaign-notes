[[Atiri]] begins training with an [[Setting/Cultures/Aeia]] Elf called [[Varma]]. Varma has been living in the woods by [[Mount Aemi]] for a long time. He says he wasn't born here however.

Atiri begins writing down his findings, discoveries and learnings in what is called the [[Atiric Texts]]. The Atiric texts are said to hold the secrets of magic that only the chosen few may use and may even hold the power to kill gods or cause [[The Convergence|catastrophes]] never before seen. These texts are all but lost now.

Varma teaches him there is more magic than just elemental magic and teaches him spells from other elements and other forms of polluted magic.

It is during this time when training with Varma he spawns the idea for creating the [[Atiri Centre of Magical Excellence]].
