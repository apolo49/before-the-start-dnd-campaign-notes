The Atiri Centre of Magical Excellence is the premier school for all magic users in all the world. It was founded in [[Solaum]] in 416 [[Dating|IOG]] by [[Atiri]], a powerful mage and philosopher. He considered the school his life's greatest accomplishment and wrote many books in there, most of which are lost.
