# Species Details

Only existing in [[Mareden]] by means of exposure to [[Walmare]], the __Umare__ are a solitary, all-female race of humanoids with equine features. The appear to be stronger, faster and are extremely charismatic.

The umare were born to run as seen in the horse-like features (ears, tails, agility). They even have joints and balance of a horse, preferring to run bent forward as opposed to upright like other humanoids. In their only settlement of Mareden they are seen entertaining the rest of the human population through dramatic derby races adorned in vibrant outfits befitting and showing each umare's unique personality and interests.

The umare are also known to have excellent singing and dancing skills, and are natural born entertainers in this regard. Following the aforementioned races, a performance not seen anywhere in the world is put on by the umare: a stage show with singing and dancing with all the runners, winners or losers.

Umare being all female can raise some questions but the reality is quite simple: they can reproduce with any-and-all humanoid races: elves, human, dwarves alike. The umare's genetics are uniquely dominant; if the child is born male they will be of the father's race, if born female they will have a high chance to be born an umare.

Umare appear as human females with some equine traits: tail and ears. Their bodies are generally lithe and athletic, optimised uniquely for speed and endurance, generally standing between 4' 9" and 5' 7", rarely reaching above 5' 9". Because of their tendency to enjoy running their builds are quite slender but muscular. Their most distinctive traits are their horse-like ears, which twitch expressively, and flowing tails that match their hair color, often styled elaborately for races and performances. Their hair comes in a variety of natural hues - chestnut, ebony, dappled gray, or golden blonde - sometimes with streaks that mimic a horse’s mane. Their eyes are bright and alert, often reflecting their vibrant personalities.

Umare have a unique digestive system that allows them to eat grass and other vegetation, though they also enjoy human foods, particularly sweets and grains. Their legs are powerful, hinting at their equine heritage, and their movements are fluid and graceful.

## Traits

__Creature Type:__ Humanoid
__Size__: Medium (4' 9" - 5' 7")
__Speed:__ 35 feet

**Ability Score Increase:** +1 to Constitution, +1 to Charisma, and +1 to Any Ability.

Race Strategy: After each Long Rest, the Uma can choose from the following:
* Front Runner: +5 to Base Speed and proficient in Acrobatics (DEX)
* Pace Chaser: +2 WIS and proficient in Perception (WIS)
* Late Surger: +2 INT and proficient in Investigation (INT)
* End Closer: +2 CON and proficient in Survival (WIS)
Note: This cannot raise an Ability Score beyond 20, remember that the bonus is lost when the strategy is changed.

**Speed Burst:** 3/Day, the Uma can _Dash_ with a Bonus Action. They regain 1 use of this after a Short Rest.

**Sturdy Haul:** If the UMA's Strength Score is at least 13, then their capacity for lifting and carrying is doubled.
