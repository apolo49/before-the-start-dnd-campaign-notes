# Premise

The players are all gathered in the capital city of [[Amphos]], in the Amphosian Despotate. On a relatively normal day, a delivery is due to reach the temple containing elemental stones and an important book to go alongside them. This is when certain individuals attempt to either guide others to the right path or intercept the waiting stones to take them for profits.

After the heist the party is driven from Amphos by the city guards. They are now due north to find a place to rest.

# Story

Set approximately 1000 years after the end of campaign 1 (in 1782 [[Dating|IOG]]), the world is now a very different place, following the migrations from the north, the [[Solara|Solaran Empire]] has fallen and fractured in to the [[Amphosian Despotate]] (de-jure and de-facto Solaran Empire, or Eastern Solaran Empire) and various other successor states including the [[New Solaran Republic]] which now controls only the [[Vincum Peninsula]] part of the region of [[Asheri]]. Despite this the influence of the Solaran Empire has lead to the spread of Solari culture, language and law leading to immense societal innovation throughout the world and has made the [[Solari (Language)|Solari language]] (once the common language of the empire) a prestige language in many places. In places where it is not, which is mainly the eastern portion of the old empire now ruled by the Amphosian Despotate, the prestige language of the old Solari Empire, [[Aeikas]], the prestige language and common language in these areas.

Because of the breakdown of many trade routes, climate changes, disease and the destruction of the institutions that the Solaran Empire once had in place, many places, cities, towns and villages have seen extreme de-population; even affecting places outside of the once great empire as logistical networks faltered. Great cities such as [[Solaum]] which once bolstered a population of 1,000,000 people now only host around 30,000 people. The largest city on the planet, and richest, is [[Amphos]] and hosts a population of around 200,000, still in the process of depopulation at this point however.
