# Premise

The party begins their morning at the [[Murotes's House|Harbormaster’s House]] in [[Aklebe]], where tensions from previous sessions linger and the reality of their mission sets in. After investigating their host’s cluttered home and learning of his desperate financial situation due to rampant gambling in the city, the party heads to [[The Panting Shadow|a local gambling den]] to win back a ship.

Inside the den, the party encounters [[Masha]], a mysterious firbolg who joins their cause after a fateful meeting. Amidst high-stakes games of "Poison Roulette" and magical cheating, their pasts begin to catch up with them. [[Ellowen]]’s secret noble identity is nearly exposed by [[Tabtab|a royal apothecary]], and the party successfully secure [[The Zoraneset|a powerful war galley]]. The session concludes with the party boarding their new vessel, preparing to sail toward the mountains with a new ally in tow.

# Story

[[Dating|3rd Hanomi 1782 IOG]], 05:30 - 15:00

## Morning at the Harbormaster's

The session opens with [[Ellowen]] waking early due to her elven trance. She discovers [[Big Beefy Bob]]'s orange hat is still glowing with a blinding magical light, a side effect of the previous session's events. The house itself is strange: windowless, triangular, and packed with maritime gear. [[Riley]] investigates the clutter and finds a painting of a warship—not a merchant vessel, but a military craft equipped with a siphon and a banner they don't recognize.

The Harbormaster ([[Murotes]]) reveals that [[Aklebe]] is a "ghost of a city" because all trade has been diverted to the capital. In his desperation and boredom, he has gambled away the city's remaining ships. He pleads with the party to go to [[The Panting Shadow]] and win [[The Zoraneset|one]] back. Before they leave, he gives them a stern warning: **"Do not play the cup game."**

## The Streets of Aklebe

As the party heads toward the gambling den, they encounter a homeless man who echoes the Harbormaster's warning, telling them to stay away from the cups. The atmosphere of the city is one of decay and desperation, contrasting with the opulence of the gambling den they are about to enter.

## The Panting Shadow

Inside the den, the party is met by a diverse crowd, including [[Masha]], a firbolg who claims she was "fated" to meet them and quickly attaches herself to the group.

At the bar, an [[Mashop|Oracle]] provides cryptic fortunes. She tells the party they will find prosperity but warns them not to burn anything down. While serving drinks, Ellowen notices the Oracle tapping a rhythmic code on the bar top—a secret communication with the bartender.

## The Gambling and The Apothecary

The party engages in several games to build their pot:

- **Blackjack & Roulette:** Using _Mage Hand_ and _Prestidigitation_, the party manipulates the cards and the dealer to win a significant amount of gold.
- **The Cup Game:** Despite the warnings, the party observes the game, realizing it is a rigged scam intended to drain travelers.
- **The Hand of Fate (Poison Roulette):** The party enters a high-stakes game involving 28 drinks, six of which are poisoned with mugwort. Big Beefy Bob and [[Yvaquis]] take the brunt of the drinking, eventually falling into a groggy, poisoned stupor.

During the games, Ellowen is approached by [[Tabtab]], [[Tukue Potes|the Prince’s]] apothecary. Tabtab, a "catfire" (Tabaxi/Half-Elf hybrid), recognizes Ellowen. She addresses her as "Lady Elowen" and makes specific references to Brackenfall and the Aurelian Keep, nearly exposing Ellowen's noble background to the rest of the party. Riley later attempts to "rizz" Tabtab to steal her diary but fails due to her sharp wit and the Prince's protection.

## Departure

Despite the poisoning and the close calls with their identities, the party wins/buys the [[The Zoraneset|Imperial War Galley]] for 50 gold. The vessel is a massive warship with 60 rowers and a prow designed to shoot Greek Fire. The session ends with the party, including their new companion Masha, boarding the ship and setting sail for the mountains.

# Story Beats

- **Morning at the Harbormaster's**: Ellowen discovers Bob's glowing hat; the party wakes to a pitch-black, windowless house.
- **House Investigation**: Riley discovers a painting of a warship with a cannon, suggesting the Harbormaster has a military or naval background.
- **Harbormaster's Plea**: Murotes explains the economic decline of Aklebe and his own gambling losses.
- **Entering the Den**: The party arrives at "The Panting Shadow" to gamble for a ship.
- **Meeting Masha**: A new ally, Masha, is introduced and identifies Ellowen as "special".
- **High-Stakes Gambling**:
    - **Blackjack**: The party uses magic and sleight of hand to win gold.
    - **Poison Roulette**: The party survives a game involving 28 drinks, six of which were poisoned.
- **Securing the Galley**: The party wins/secures a war galley equipped with a fire-blast prow.
- **Departure**: The party and Masha board the ship to head toward the mountains.
