# Premise

The party take on a small job clearing a tavern storage room of rats in exchange for a promised letter. What appears to be a simple task quickly escalates into excessive violence, improvised magic, and collateral damage to the tavern’s produce.

While completing the job, the party uncovers a letter detailing pirate activity near [[Aklebe]] and the existence of a [[The Zoraneset|well-armed trading war galley]] heading toward [[Mount Aemi]]. This information establishes their next destination and hints at greater dangers beyond the tavern.

Further exploration of the inn reveals locked guest rooms, signs of religious secrecy, stolen personal belongings, and the presence of a mysterious traveller. Through conversation and careful observation, one party member realizes this individual is far more important than he appears — a royal traveling incognito on a diplomatic mission.

The session transitions the party from survival-level adventuring into political and maritime intrigue.

# Story

[[Dating|2nd Hanomi 1782 IOG]], 06:00 - 12:30

The party awake at the tavern seeking a letter and are told they must first clear rats from the establishment’s storage room. After unlocking the door, they discover the infestation consists of several giant rats hiding among food crates.

Combat quickly becomes chaotic. One rat is brutally dismembered, splattering blood across apples and crates. Another is magically desiccated into dust through necrotic magic. The final rat survives long enough to injure [[Big Beefy Bob]] before being chilled, stabbed, and finished off by [[Riley]], who is eager to finally perform thier role in combat.

With the rats dealt with, the party rummage through the storage room and discover a letter discussing pirate attacks on ships near [[Aklebe]]. The letter also grants passage aboard [[The Zoraneset|a heavily armed trading war galley]] heading toward [[Itrea]], establishing [[Aklebe]] as the party’s next destination.

Exploring the upstairs rooms, the party break into several guest chambers. One room belongs to a wealthy and educated occupant, containing maps, writing implements, and an illegal religious idol. The idol is identified but left behind to avoid future consequences. A hidden lute is discovered and taken, creating an unspoken debt between party members.

During this time, Riley takes his first bath in a very long while, emerging significantly cleaner, though only marginally more respectable.

Back in the common room, the party interact with a [[Tukue Potes|quiet, well-dressed traveller posing as a poor man]]. Through shared food, awkward conversation, and careful investigation, [[Ellowen]] recognizes the man as the Prince of [[Amphos]] traveling in disguise. He is on a diplomatic mission to the holy city-state of [[Itrea]]. Ellowen keeps this revelation secret, suspecting both danger and opportunity if the truth were revealed.

The session ends with the party having secured their letter, identified their next destination, and unknowingly becoming entangled in royal diplomacy.
