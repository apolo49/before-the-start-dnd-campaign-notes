# Premise

The party continue their journey north toward [[Aklebe]], traveling through quiet countryside marked by ancient graves, forgotten stonework, and signs that the land itself holds deep elemental significance.

What begins as uneventful travel turns meaningful when the party discover a broken tombstone and a nearby stone circle tied to an [[Hasharshagetes|ancient traveler]] and [[Bon|long-lost peoples]]. These discoveries hint that the world’s elemental forces were once understood — and interacted with — very differently.

The journey takes a violent turn when the party are ambushed by wolves in the forest, forcing them into a desperate fight while already worn down from travel. In the aftermath of the battle, the party encounter an ancient golden tree growing from magically charged ground. Though the tree itself is not magical, interaction with it reveals powerful truths about elemental forces, the land, and the party’s growing connection to both.

The session ends with the party reaching [[Aklebe]], battered, altered, and carrying knowledge they do not yet understand.

# Story

[[Dating|2nd Hanomi 1782 IOG]], 13:00 - 20:30

The party travel north along the road toward [[Aklebe]], passing through open meadows filled with burrows, flowers, and scattered trees. The land is quiet but clearly well-used. Along the roadside, they discover a broken tombstone partially buried in overgrowth. Investigation reveals the name _[[Hasharshagetes]]_ carved in an ancient language.

Through discussion and lore, the party recall stories of Hasharshagetes, a legendary traveller and observer from a distant age, associated with early understanding of the world’s elemental forces and the cycle of existence. The tombstone appears less like a grave and more like a marker.

Nearby, the party discover an ancient stone circle attributed to an early people known as the _[[Bon]]_. The stones are covered in mosses of varying colours, each corresponding to known [[Elements|elemental aspects]]. Arcane investigation reveals the area is charged with latent energy, though little can be safely activated. The party note the significance of the site before continuing onward.

As the road narrows and forest thickens, the party begin to notice signs of animal movement. Before they can react, they are ambushed by a pack of wolves emerging from the undergrowth.

Combat is brutal and chaotic. [[Big Beefy Bob]] absorbs repeated attacks and is left badly injured. [[Yvaquis]] destroys one wolf with divine power, while others are brought down through force and magic. The party survive the ambush, but several members are left in critical condition.

In the aftermath, the group stop to tend wounds. Medicine checks clean injuries but do not restore strength. Food and alcohol provide limited comfort. Eventually, magical healing is used to stabilise the party enough to continue.

After several more hours of travel, the party encounter a fork in the path. One route continues forward, while a faint side path leads deeper into the forest. Investigation reveals a strange, ancient golden tree growing in a nearby clearing, and curiosity draws the party toward it.

The golden tree is enormous and impossibly old, estimated to be thousands of years in age. Investigation reveals that the tree itself is not magical; instead, its roots draw power from magically charged stones deep beneath the forest floor. The surrounding land is subtly altered as a result.

Each party member experiences a different reaction when interacting with the tree:

- [[Riley]] finds that alcohol instantly loses its effect and the tree starts burning when he touches it.
- [[Ellowen]] experiences [teleportation via means of turning into water](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Misty%20Step#content).
- [[Yvaquis]] experiences overwhelming elemental communion, sensing the planet as an interconnected whole and briefly reshaping the terrain beneath her feet. The earth deforms around her feet.
- [[Big Beefy Bob]] experiences repeated visions through the eyes of a long-dead figure, witnessing mountains, altars, sealed gates, and the containment of catastrophic elemental power and historical understanding tied to long-dead travellers and truths of the land.

Attempts to combine elemental stones with the tree result in unpredictable and dangerous effects, including teleportation, heat, and damage to the tree itself. Casting _Identify_ reveals the tree acts as a conduit and amplifier for elemental power rather than a source. Overuse causes the tree to become unresponsive, and the party eventually decide to stop experimenting.

Returning to the road, the party continue toward [[Aklebe]]. Along the way, they notice enormous footprints filled with water near the roadside and a felled tree blocking further travel north-east but choose not to investigate further.

Upon arrival in [[Aklebe]], the party meet the [[Murotes|Harbour Master]], who offers them [[Murotes's House|lodging]] and proposes a job involving reclaiming [[The Zoraneset|his ship]] through gambling at the [[Panting Shadow]]. Failure would incur a penalty, though this is reduced through negotiation.

That night, the party rest. Most sleep normally, but [[Big Beefy Bob]] experiences disturbing dreams in which whispering voices offer power and [[Atiric Texts|Ellowen's book]] speaks cryptically of memory, loss, and forgotten names. During the night, [[Aushuzi|a magical mishap]] causes [[Big Beefy Bob's Little Orange Hat]] to glow brightly, unnoticed by the others.

The session ends with the party resting in [[Aklebe]], their understanding of the world — and themselves — fundamentally altered.
