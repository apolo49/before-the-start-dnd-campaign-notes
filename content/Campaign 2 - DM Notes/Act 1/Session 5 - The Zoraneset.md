# Premise

The party begins their journey aboard [[The Zoraneset]], the bireme Imperial war dromon won in the previous session. As they navigate the open waters toward the mountains, the party must adjust to life at sea, managing a massive crew and a captain with mysterious motives. The voyage is quickly interrupted by a supernatural fog and a harrowing encounter with a vessel, the vessel of the Prince's ship through the fog. Amidst the chaos of naval combat, [[Big Beefy Bob]]’s connection to the "Dead Dreams" deepens, revealing cryptic warnings from a singular, clear voice that identifies itself as the "First Puli."

# Story

## Life Aboard The Zoraneset

The party familiarizes themselves with [[The Zoraneset]], a 120-foot by 25-foot imperial warship equipped with ballistas and a front-mounted fire siphon. They meet [[Mari Shkari|Captain Mari Shkari]], who informs them that out of a required 200 crew members, they only have 150, meaning the party must step up and assume officer roles.

The party divides the responsibilities:

- [[Masha]] becomes the Ship's Cook
- [[Elowen]] takes on the role of Quartermaster to navigate the ship. Riley secretly hands her a stolen map in the bathroom to aid her checks.
- [[Big Beefy Bob]] is appointed First Mate, tasked with keeping crew morale high. He refuses to take off his glowing orange hat despite the captain's complaints.
- [[Riley]] assumes the role of Bosun, heading below deck to repair holes and broken oars.
- [[Yvaquis]] falls into the role of the ship's Surgeon.

## Masha's Secrets and Royal Slips

While chatting, Masha mentions she is from [[Eivia]] and brings up a missing princess named Evangeline. Yvaquis accidentally responds to the name, heavily hinting to the party that she is the runaway princess.

Later, Mari Shkari tells Masha about a rumour from [[The Panting Shadow|the gambling den]]: black powder was used to blow a hole beneath the [[The Great Palace of Amphos|royal keep]] in [[Amphos]]. The prime suspect was seen wearing a worn, bright woollen cloak and looked like a sheep, a perfect description of Masha, a Firbolg. Despite her nervous demeanour, Masha denies involvement, and Ellowen's insight check fails to glean her true intentions.

## The Water Plane and The Octopus

While on deck, [[The Water Stone|Ellowen's magical stone]] begins to speak to her in a strange voice (jokingly compared to Jar-Jar Binks). It pulls her into a trance where she walks on an endless ocean in the water plane. An indescribable eldritch beast appears, warning her that [[Second Convergence|"The seven have become the six"]] and instructing her to complete [[The Ascension|"the ascension"]] by climbing [[Mount Aemi|the mountain]] in [[Itrea]].

The trance is violently interrupted by the sound of suction as a Giant Octopus attacks the ship. The beast immediately smashes the ship's oars, killing several rowers and dealing massive damage to the hull.

- **Riley** orders the remaining crew to start patching the hull while he fires a flaming arrow—lit by his own magical rock—into the beast's tentacle.
- **Elowen** blasts it with a _Firebolt_.
- **Yvaquis** damages it with her _Divine Spark_ channel divinity.
- **Big Beefy Bob** enters a rage, heavily damaging the octopus with his axes. In a bizarre final blow, Bob attempts to give the terrified octopus a hug while holding his axes, inadvertently executing the creature.

## Ancient Legends and Dead Dreams

That evening, the party eats the octopus, magically flavoured by Ellowen. Captain Mari Shkari shares an ancient legend about a party of seven elementals who travelled the world (including [[Babaren]]), attuned to obelisks, and almost toppled an empire. He warns them that the [[Solara|The Republic of Solara]] used to execute [[Elementals|elementals]], prompting the party to realize the danger of their newly chosen group name, "The Elementals".

The captain assigns night watches, and the party goes to sleep. In his slumber, Big Beefy Bob enters the "Dead Dreams," where a singular, clear voice introduces itself as the "[[Puli Numini|First Puli]].". It promises to help him if he listens. Meanwhile, [[Atiric Texts|a cryptic book]] speaks into Ellowen's mind about the "Bruno walls" and the tearing of the "Margos".

At 4:00 AM, the crewmate [[Dashos]] fails to show up for his watch. At 5:00 AM, the party is jolted awake by a sudden, terrifying male scream, ending the session.

# Story Beats

- **Assuming Command:** The party takes on officer roles to manage the understaffed _Zoraneset_.
- **Masha's Suspicions:** Masha is heavily implicated in a terrorist explosion at the Amphos royal keep.
- **The Royal Secret:** Yvaquis accidentally reacts to her real name, Evangeline Marquis, outing herself to the group.
- **The Trance:** A magical stone pulls Ellowen into the water plane, where an eldritch being warns that "the seven have become the six".
- **The Octopus Attack:** A Giant Octopus attacks the ship, killing rowers and breaking oars before the party slays it.
- **Lore Drop:** The captain recounts a myth of seven ancient elementals who nearly destroyed an empire.
- **The First Pooley:** Bob receives a clear, prophetic message in his dreams from an entity offering help.
- **Cliffhanger:** A crewman goes missing, and a male scream echoes across the ship at 5:00 AM.
