This is the story of how John Ironchest joined the party of the Elementals and discovered he is the power elemental. He begins at his mountain home and makes his way south through mountains, forests, towns, villages and rivers to arrive at [[Pithetara]]. This is where he participates in a [[World Bodybuilding Order|WBO]] competition and gets spotted by the elementals in the crowd.

## Story

### Leaving Akrossa
06:00 - 07:30

John wakes up in a tavern built into the ruins of an old Solari granary. The city of 1,254 is waking up; the smell of roasting chickpeas and damp river fog fills the air.

A local military official is seizing horses or supplies for a nearby skirmish. To leave [[Akrossa]] with his gear intact, John must either show his "Soldier’s Seal" (which can be gained from another tavern goer) or perform a feat of strength to prove he’s more useful as a free agent than a conscript.

A thief tries to snatch John’s great-axe in the crowded market. John lets them run five paces before summoning the weapon back to his hand, leaving the thief grasping at air.

### Byblarascu
11:00 - 12:00

Blessing from the God of Strength, [[Harakishi]] at his temple. A priest notices John’s Eldritch mark. He offers a vial of Holy Water or a Spell Scroll of Shield if John can "bless" the local guard’s practice weapons using his Weapon Bond ritual, which the locals view as a divine miracle.

#### Byblarascu to Pyrma
12:00 - 13:00

The road narrows and becomes overgrown. John is stalked by **2 Scouts** (local bandits) who think a lone traveler is an easy mark.

John can use _Prestigiditation_ to create the sound of marching boots behind him, tricking the scouts into thinking he has backup, or simply tank their opening volleys with a high AC.

### Pyrma
13:00 - 14:00

A tiny, muddy hamlet of 50 people. Everyone knows everyone. They are suspicious of "Ironchest."

The village Headman, a weary man named Basil, explains that the boat John is looking for isn't just "lost"—it’s drifted into the **Reeds of the Weeping Woman**.

John must navigate the local politics. The villagers believe the boat is cursed. He has one hour to gather supplies (oars, pitch, rope) before heading to the water.

#### Recover the Boat

The boat is wedged in a thicket of razor-grass.

Mud Mephit has made a nest in the hull. John must clear the creature without damaging the boat.

Once cleared, John uses his Strength (Athletics) to shove the boat into the main current.

### Boat Trip to Pithertara
14:00 - 15:30


#### 1. The Toll of the Chain-Breakers

**The Setup:** A group of river bandits has stretched a thick, rusted iron chain just beneath the water's surface between two Roman-era bridge ruins.

- **The Conflict:** As the boat hits the chain, the player must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** to avoid being tossed overboard.
    
- **EK Spotlight:** The bandits fire crossbows from the ruins. This is the perfect moment to use the **Shield** spell to deflect bolts while using **Weapon Bond** to "summon" a stowed spear or bow to return fire.
    
- **Goal:** Use an Athletics check to pry the boat over the chain or a well-placed _Fire Bolt_ to ignite the wooden winch on the shore.
    

#### 2. The "Sunken Arch" Obstacle

**The Setup:** The river narrows at a collapsed Byzantine aqueduct. The 345 m³/s discharge creates a "white water" effect, forcing the boat toward a jagged stone pillar.

- **The Conflict:** This is a "Skill Challenge." The player needs to navigate the boat.
- **EK Spotlight:** If the boat is about to crash, the player can use their action to cast **Blade Ward** to resistance-tank the impact, or use **Expeditious Retreat** (if known) to frantically paddle out of the vortex.
- **The Twist:** A **Gray Ooze** is clinging to the underside of the arch and drops onto the boat as it passes. The player must fight in the cramped space of the boat without tipping it.

#### 3. The Cargo Retrieval

**The Setup:** The player spots a half-sunken Byzantine transport boat wedged against a sandbar. A survivor is clinging to a crate.

- **The Conflict:** The current is moving at 18 km/h relative to the shore. Once the player passes the wreck, they can't easily row back up-stream.
- **EK Spotlight:** This tests the **Weapon Bond** feature in a creative way. The player can throw their bonded weapon (tied to a rope) into the crate or the wreck, and then use the "Summon" feature to essentially create a magical grappling hook to anchor themselves or pull an item toward them.
- If saved, the compliments John Ironchest and gives him a **potion of healing**, and the crate contains rum, some spell scrolls, some herbs and some rum, as well as some googly eyes, it also contains a [[The History of Tabaxi|torn copy of a manuscript about the history of tabaxi]].
