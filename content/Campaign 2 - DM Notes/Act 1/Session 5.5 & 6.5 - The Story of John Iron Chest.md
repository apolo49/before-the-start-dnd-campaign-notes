# Session 6.5

## Premise

John Ironchest, a travelling strongman and competitor of the World Bodybuilding Order, departs the town of Akrossa on a journey to reach a competition in Pithertara. Along the way he acquires a powerful warhorse, hires a young orphan named Ulriki as his squire, and demonstrates extraordinary physical strength to overcome practical obstacles. Travelling through settlements including Byblarascu and Pyrma, John encounters religious institutions, suspicious villagers, wilderness threats, and logistical challenges. After recovering a stranded riverboat and navigating hazards along the river, John ultimately reaches Pithertara just in time to enter the bodybuilding competition. The story establishes John’s character as a sceptical, physically dominant traveller navigating a world shaped by remnants of Solari civilisation, while forming the beginnings of a partnership with his new squire.

## Story

### Leaving Akrossa
06:00 - 08:00

[[John Ironchest|John]] wakes up in a tavern built into the ruins of an old Solari granary. The city of 1,254 is waking up; the smell of roasting chickpeas and damp river fog fills the air.

John makes his way downstairs talks to the tavern-keep, who laments that the grain that is stored outside is too heavy to move (400kg/barrel), and that the horses next door at the stables keep eating the grain out of the barrels when they inevitably break. John takes up the task of moving the barrels, he carries all 5 at once, moving 2Mg of grain at once. The tavern-keep was impressed by this show of strength and skill he compliments John saying he could hang off his arms. John remembers that the horses are at the stables and asks the tavern-keep if he could take a horse, the tavern-keep wants the horse off his hands and allows the purchase. There are 5 horses for sale:

- [[Come-At-Me White]]
- Silverboat
- Akrossa's Bottom Fist
- Ordinary Day
- Army's Call

After observing all the horses, John deduces that Come-At-Me White was the strongest and largest horse. The tavern-keep says he used to work for the [[Imperial Army of the Eastern Solaran Empire|Imperial Army of Amphos]] and that the horse was sired from an imperial war-horse. Come-At-Me White is a 4 year old, very dark bay, stallion, weighing approximately 550 kg, and stands at 17 hands high; he has a narrow white blaze. He selects this horse to be his and the tavern keeper says to have him with no payment, he also purchases some grain to carry with him to feed the stallion.

When saddling the horse he discovers the saddle doesn't fit and that the reins, bridle and bits are also too small with the mouse-piece causing the stallion extreme discomfort, hurting the horses mouth and face.

He goes back to the tavern-keep after undressing the horse and queries if there is a leather-worker or tannery in the city. The tavern-keep indulges John with the information required, saying that the leather-worker used to work for the imperial army and has retired to here. John makes haste to the leather-worker.

The leather-worker, [[Basil Ceyx]], confirms that he worked as a leather-worker for the Imperial East Solari army, making armour and leather items for the cataphracts. He claims he works with the finest leather and uses the finest tanning techniques and finest metals. He examines the saddle, and other fittings for the horse and says the fittings were made for a much smaller mare, not at all 17 hands tall and weighing much under 500kg. Saying he will need to create brand new fittings, but not to fret as he will make them in under 25 minutes as he is extremely skilled, it will only cost John 40 gold. John argues this is expensive and that the [[World Bodybuilding Order|WBO]] is the perfect place to advertise this saddle. Basil says he is already offering a discount and is rushing, the price should have been 50 gold. John says he will give a personal endorsement at his next show in [[Pithertara]], a city of 8158 people, if he pays just 20 gold. Basil accepts 21 gold, making it at a loss as he understands that John must have it and he expects a massive endorsement, also making him a leather sponsor patch. When inspecting it even John, an untrained eye, can see this is saddle is of exceptional quality, made in the imperial style. 

John goes to leave and when passing the square gets approached by [[Ulriki|a young, impoverished, malnourished lad]]. To John he looks 12 years old. As he approached the man on the horse he begged for food and bread. John asks the young man where his family was and if he has any parents, the young man says they are poor and live in the slums. John tells the young man to follow him and goes to the tavern where he buys the kid a meal and chats with him. John asks the tavern keep who says he recognises the boy as poor and a beggar but does not know his name nor where he came from, apparently he appeared here a couple months ago. John then proceeds to interrogate the young boy discovering he is from [[Lqlima]], has no parents, no money and no job opportunities. John takes pity upon the boy and offers him a job for 2 silver pieces per day as his squire before even discovering his name. The boy notices this and tells him his name is *Ulriki*, a [[Setting/Cultures/Solari|Solari]] name, but John is unbeknownst to this fact. 

John sets off, still sceptical of the boy and clutches his possessions closely, and mounts his horse and saddle, he also sets his squire on the horse, bareback.

#### Akrossa to Byblarascu
08:00 - 09:30

While trotting along the path, the horse is alerted to the rhythmic thump of a beast hopping across the path ahead, John sets the horse to a gallop and avoids being encountered by a Giant Toad. Which crosses the path behind them unawares.

### Byblarascu
09:30 - 10:30

John approaches the gates of the city and talks to the guard who directs him to the temple of [[Harakishi]] in the city and explains he is the patron god of the city. John is not impressed by this as he sees the god as a proxy of strength but not as a representation of pure, unfiltered, internal strength.  

He goes to the temple anyway to receive a blessing for his weak squire, Ulriki. Ulriki receives the blessing then feels much better, energised and more confident. He becomes a devotee of Harakishi.

A priest notices John's extreme strength and gives him holy water in return for blessing the local guard's practice weapons. John refuses but takes the holy water anyway as he does not believe in Harakishi and does not understand the fascination with magic and the divine.

He also heard form Ulriki about the pillar in the city square. He asks the priest about this who tells him it is a replica of the [[Obelisk of Origin]] in the Eternal City of [[Solaum]]. He learns that here the Obelisk of Origin is called the Obelisk of Power.

Pleased with this information he proceeds to remount his horse with his squire, and make way to Pyrma.

#### Byblarascu to Pyrma
10:30 - 11:00

The road narrows and becomes overgrown. John notices that he is being stalked by at least 1 Scouts. He again asks Come-At-Me White to gallop and out paces the scouts.

### Pyrma
11:00 - 12:00

John arrives in [[Pyrma]] with his small travelling band and is immediately met with suspicion by the local residents. Ulriki shares this feeling and also states that he has been here before on his travels stating that "The first time I was here, everyone was unwelcoming and suspicious of me as I travelled through here. I left as soon as I came." Ulriki explains that it is severely undeveloped and only has a population of 50.

The village headman is approached by John who for a boat. The headman laughs and retorts:
	"we only have one and it's stuck!"
	"It's in the Reeds of the Weeping Woman. Everyone who goes there ends up crying and returning."

John must navigate the local politics. The villagers believe the boat is cursed. He has one hour to gather supplies (oars, pitch, rope) before heading to the water.

#### Recover the Boat

The boat is wedged in a thicket of razor-grass and mangrove roots.

John finds this laughable and leaps 30ft across the river. He lands just short of the boat where he is attacked. A *Mud Mephit* has made a nest in the hull. He launches the creature into the river where the fast current carries it away.

John passed the mephit with ease and wondered what everyone was making a fuss about.

He attempts to pull it out using the anchor. The chain for the anchor snaps due to rust and deterioration from being stuck for so long without maintenance.

After clearing it with help from the village people, who Ulriki brought along to help after some socialisation with the farmer, John dry docks the boat by pulling it to shore; acting as an anchor, he allows Come-At-Me White, Ulriki and the anyone who wants passage to Pithertara to board. He then pulls the boat out to the river and hastens the journey by pulling it most of the way.

### Boat Trip to Pithertara
12:00 - 13:30


#### 1. The Toll of the Chain-Breakers

A group of river bandits has stretched a thick, rusted iron chain just beneath the water's surface between two Solari-era bridge ruins.

- **The Conflict:** As the boat hits the chain, the player must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** to avoid being tossed overboard.
- The bandits fire crossbows from the ruins.
- John Ironchest intimidates them and drives them away

#### 2. The "Sunken Arch" Obstacle

The river narrows at a collapsed Byzantine aqueduct. The 345 m³/s discharge creates a "white water" effect, forcing the boat toward a jagged stone pillar.

- This is a "Skill Challenge." The player needs to navigate the boat.
- John failed to avoid the pillar and gives in.
- John hangs self over boat to tank the aqueduct pillar, ready to beat the pillar and save the ship
- Ulkiri steers boat of the way of the pillar.

#### 3. The Cargo Retrieval

The player spots a half-sunken, razed Byzantine transport boat wedged against a sandbar. A survivor is clinging to a crate.

- The current is moving at 18 km/h relative to the shore. Once the player passes the wreck, they can't easily row back up-stream.
- John leaps from the boat once in range, throws the survivor and grabs the rope attached to the crate and leaps before the boat passes the wreck.
- The victim compliments John Ironchest and gives him a **potion of healing**, and allows him to take the crate which contains rum, some spell scrolls, some herbs, as well as some googly eyes, it also contains a [[The History of Tabaxi|torn copy of a manuscript about the history of tabaxi]].

### Arrival at Pithertara
John, on the anchorless cog borrowed from the hamlet of Pyrma, arrived in Pithertara, he sailed down the river to the mouth and docked at the harbour. This is where he heard news about [[The Zoraneset]] and someone being killed and someone else being arrested. As he only had 15 minutes to make it to the WBO competition, he ran across the city, dropped off his belongings, stabled his horse and bought a room in an inn for both him and Ulriki. He then registered his attendance at the competition and walked onto the stage.
