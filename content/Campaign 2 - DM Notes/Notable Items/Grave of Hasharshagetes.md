# Text on the Grave
**Hasharshagetes**
"He who saw the dawn of an ancient age is now a seed of the night."

# The Myth
In the grey-light of the First **Za**, when the names of the stars were yet unwritten and the gods had not yet carved their laws into the heavens, there walked **Haʃarʃagetes**. He was a man of the [[Bon]]. He was a soul who refused the power of a lord and the earthen promises of the house-master. While others sought the warmth of the fires of the hearth, he sought only the edge of the world.

"I shall be the breath of [[Aushuzi|the dawn]]," he declared to the silent stones. With a bronze blade, he shaped the boat, a vessel of fir to brave the wakes, and committed his fate to the seas. He sailed where the salt-spray stings with the fury of [[Shuzi|Dash]], crossing the vast, standing waters where the deep-gods dream. He was the first to track the tamagase - the spirit-signs - written in the foam and the flight of the white-birds.

Beyond the horizon’s veil, he reached the [[Mount Aemi|Zama]], the peak that hold the gates to the heavens above. He climbed until the air grew thin as a ghost’s whisper, higher than the bear dares to tread. All that accompanied him was the whispers of the zephyrs. There, in the silence of the high peaks, the veil was rent; waters succumbed to ice. He did not find jewels; he found **Ma**. He saw the Great Reality, the shimmering thread that binds the seed to the star, and the fires to the sun. In that moment of blinding light, he was no longer a traveler; he was the shadotor - the Seer who knows the weight of the world.

He left [[Elements|the seven]] where they should remain. On descent the man in white watched only observed in the corner of an eye.

But the night comes for every-one even those who walk the path of the shadotor. When his hands grew too weary to grasp the oar, he steered his boat home. He dropped his heavy anchor into the silt of the ancestral bay, not in surrender, but in completion. He laid his head upon the earth and became the very thing he had witnessed: a fragment of the cycle.

Now he rests beneath unmoving stone.

- **First Za** = first era, before the first convergence
- **Hakimu** = heavens
- **Zama** = mountain
- **Dash** = Sky god, king of the gods, Zeus, Jupiter, Dyeus Pater
- **Ma** = reality, truth
