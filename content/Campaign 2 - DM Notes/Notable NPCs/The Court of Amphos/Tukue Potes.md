# Backstory
**Tukue Potes** is a high-ranking official and the primary administrator of the Lower Court, the secluded residence of the [[Bardashes|Emperor]]’s consorts. While he initially presents himself as a high-status eunuch, his true identity, as the Heir to the Throne of the [[Eastern Solaran Empire]], and physical status remain one of the most guarded secrets within the Imperial Court. Known for his "celestial beauty," Tukue Potes uses his appearance as both a shield and a weapon to maintain order in a den of political vipers.

## Administrator of the Lower Court
Tukue Potes functions as the "manager" of the Emperor's women. He discovers [[Tabtab]] after she solves the mystery of the dying imperial infants and quickly recognizes her unique value. Throughout this period, Tukue Potes is characterized by his "honeyed mask"—a façade of perfect, radiant charm that he uses to manipulate consorts and officials alike. However, he grows increasingly frustrated by Tabtab’s unique immunity to his charms, which eventually blossoms into a genuine, albeit awkward, romantic fixation. His primary role involves mitigating the "Honey Poison" incident and managing the fallout of the attempted assassination of Consort Rishi.

## Reveal and Ritual
During a strategic hunting trip, his true physical nature - that he is not a eunuch, but an "intact" man - is discovered by Tabtab. This revelation clarifies his true status as the Imperial Brother (and potential heir), a role he finds stifling.

His administrative brilliance during his trial of the Shrine of Choosing. Tukue Potes is tasked with navigating the ritualistic and architectural puzzles of the imperial ancestors. While he relies on Tabtab’s scientific deductions to solve the "miracle" of the coloured doors, it is his political manoeuvring that allows the Emperor to maintain face and authority during the visiting envoys' stay.

# Personality and Relationships
Tukue Potes is a man of contradictions: a powerful statesman who is secretly insecure, and a "celestial" figure who acts like a petulant child when his plans are foiled. His relationship with his aide, Gotinos, provides the emotional backbone of his life, as Gotinos is one of the few people who treats him as a human rather than an icon. Tukue Potes's interest in Tabtab has shifted from professional curiosity to a deep-seated need for her presence, even as he realises that bringing her closer to him puts her in the crosshairs of imperial politics.
