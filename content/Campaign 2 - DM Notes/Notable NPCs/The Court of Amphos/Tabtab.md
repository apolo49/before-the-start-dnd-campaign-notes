# Backstory
**Tabtab** is a 17-year-old half-tabaxi/half-elf (catfire) whose life is defined by a deep expertise in medicinal arts and a penchant for solving complex puzzles, fitting the mould of an apothecary. Standing at a petite height and slender weight , she possesses dark hair and fair skin. She frequently uses makeup or clay to mask her features, opting to appear "plain" or sickly to avoid unwanted attention. Perhaps her most striking physical characteristic is the collection of scars lining her arms, remnants of her obsessive habit of testing various toxins and poisons on her own body.

## Early Life and Training
Born in the bustling Pleasure District of [[Amphos]] in the [[Eastern Solaran Empire]], Tabtab was raised by her foster father, a former court physician. Under his mentorship, she developed a scientific worldview, prioritising empirical evidence and cause-and-effect over faith in the divine or destiny. During this time she was working as an apprentice apothecary for the premier Brothel in the district. This also happens to be where her mother, who is a victim of  syphilis due to becoming less valued after having Tabtab, resides in secret, out of view. Her father [[Zenshoran Hagashar]] frequents here attempting to buy both Tabtab and her mother, but his patronage is rejected due to his inability to buy Tabtab's mother earlier and Tabtab's hatred of him.

The education she received from her father and the brothel provided her with the skills necessary to navigate both the herbalist's shop and the dangerous political landscapes she would later inhabit. Her quiet life of gathering herbs was interrupted when she was kidnapped by traffickers and sold into domestic service within the Imperial Court of Amphos.

## Initial Service in the Court
Throughout her initial appointment at the court she is discovered by [[Tukue Potes]], as someone who is more educated than the rest of the servants and had helped prevent a miscarriage and death of the consort to the emperor [[Bardashes]]. She was hired by one of the consort as one of her ladies-in-waiting, throughout this time she grew closer to Tukue Potes and has been noticed by him and her services requested when there was a problem he could not solve alone and required extensive intrigue and courtly gossip.

Eventually she was fired in a court-wide shakeup.

## Return to the Court
After her initial dismissal, Tabtab is brought back to the Court by Tukue Potes. Her primary mission in is to serve as the chief food taster and protector for a Consort, whose second pregnancy must be kept a strictly guarded secret to avoid the political backstabbing. During this time, Tabtab acts as the first line of defence against the constant threat of assassination and medical negligence targeting the imperial heir.

## Understanding Tukue Potes
The most significant shift in Tabtab's history occurs during a royal hunt. While attending to Tukue Potes, she discovers the secret he has been hiding behind the façade of a eunuch: he is an intact male. This discovery forces her to confront the reality of the prince's true identity as the heir (or potential protender) to the throne of Amphos. Despite her desire to remain an indifferent observer, she is now burdened with a secret that could lead to the execution of everyone involved.

# Personality and Disposition
Tabtab is a dedicated introvert who generally avoids social interaction unless it is necessary for her work or safety. In conversation, she is known for a direct, dry, and often blunt delivery. While she is content to act as a follower and maintain a façade of respect toward authority, she internally challenges any ideas she finds logically unsound. She is not motivated by money or status, but rather by the pursuit of knowledge and the safety afforded by her group. Her greatest vice is an insatiable curiosity regarding poisons, which often overrides her sense of self-preservation.

## Ambitions and Outlook
In the short term, Tabtab is driven by the acquisition of rare medicinal ingredients and alchemical knowledge. Her broader, long-term goals involve advancing the field of medicine and securing a future where she can pursue her research freely, far removed from the high-stakes tyranny of courtly life. Despite her cynical exterior, she possesses a Neutral Good alignment, often helping those in need - especially when a medical mystery is involved - even if she claims to do so only for the sake of the puzzle.
