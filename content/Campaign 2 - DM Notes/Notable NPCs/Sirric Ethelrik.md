# Biography

Born 24 years ago in the mountainous county of [[Armth]], Sirric Ethelrik was heir to the count of Armth, Derred Ethelrik. He grew up in the keep of the capital of Armth, within the comfort of a noble family of modest wealth. His mother died by the hands of [[Eastern Solaran Empire|Amphosian]] raiders at age 10. A year later, seeing the weakness in the county, an occupation force from the [[Brackenfall|Duchy of Brackenfall]] entered the county, resulting in its annexation. The violent event created a land border between Brackenfall and the Amphosian Despotate. The Duchy of Brackenfall then razed his city, his home. It is yet to recover to its former state.

Feeling oppressed under unfamiliar and illegitimate rule, his father began fomenting a rebellion to restore the sovereignty of the county and his house.

At the age of 14, by the order of the Duke of Brackenfall, Sirric was forced to watch as his father and all adults found complicit, or otherwise aiding, the rebellion, were executed. In the same horrible moment, a dragon - whether mysteriously summoned, or drawn by the bloodshed remains unknown - attacked the capital of Brackenfall. After unleashing hell-fire upon the rebels and their families, it was killed upon a city spire. Sirric and the other children survived, though each bore burn scars along their arms.

Following this, Sirric was personally punished for being the son of the leader of the rebellion and was shunned throughout the dukedom. Everywhere he went, his name was spoken as a slur.

At the age of 16 he was conscripted to join the Brackenfallian elite forces, a collection of mage-knights designed to crush enemy incursions quickly and decisively. Despite his hatred for all things Brackenfallian, he was an exceptional soldier and earned several promotions with haste. Yet, he remains an outsider, obeyed for his rank, but never fully accepted, even by those he has aided. He was eventually granted his own battalion composed of his former kin, the children of the rebellion, officially called the "Patushepatir" (an [[Aeikas]] word borrowed from the Amphosian Despotate meaning a quarter quadrant), as they are the fourth battalion of the elite forces.

Now, at age 24 he has heard news that the [[Ellowen|heir to the Duchy of Brackenfall]] is to be betrothed to the [[Tukue Potes|Prince of the Amphosian Despotate]]. As he and his battalion march to [[Amphos]] for their diplomatic assignment to protect and escort the prince and his royal apothecary on his way to the holy city of [[Itrea]].

# Appearance

Sirric is a tall man (6' 0") with black hair and dark eyebrows. His facial features are marked by a strong jawline, and his skin is olive in colour. He often has dark stubble on his face and his body is muscular. There is a lethal grace to him. His eyes are distinctive, characterized by a shade of onyx speckled with flecks of gold. The dragon left a burn scar which starts at his left wrist and stretches all the way up his neck, stopping at his jawline. This has turned into black, almost like a tattoo. His voice has a rough pitch, but sounding like velvet. His lips are perfect. Sirric’s chest is massive, with wide shoulders and heavily muscled arms alongside. Every edge of his body is honed like a weapon. He smells of mint and leather and something that borders between citrus and floral.

# Personality

Sirric is a kind person at heart, but he has been forced to become a grim, success-oriented killer in a world that views him as a threat. All of his experiences have forced him to hide vulnerabilities and suppress emotions wherever he can. He keeps intense control over his feelings, rarely allowing others to see any sign of weakness or uncertainty. He approaches every interaction defensively, especially when he’s speaking with those in power or Brackenfallians. This emotional barrier tends to initially make Sirric appear detached and unapproachable. He is a secretive individual, closely guarding his own secrets, even from those he trusts. He places his trust in very few people and likes to keep it that way. He doesn’t hold as much faith in the rules, especially those set by the Brackenfall government, which is why he likes to command his own battalion his way. His disregard for these regulations is evident when he defies the law by meeting with more than three other children marked for rebellion outside of designated hours.
